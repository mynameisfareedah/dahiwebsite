About DAHI

Many women and young girls grow up without clear, reliable education about their own bodies. Questions about menstrual health, fertility, pregnancy, menopause, and emotional wellbeing are often left unanswered or surrounded by confusion and stigma. DAHI exists to change that.

DAHI is a women’s health education and empowerment initiative focused on providing accessible, clear, and supportive health information to women and young girls.

Mission & Vision

Mission

“To educate, empower, and support women and young girls by providing accessible and trustworthy health knowledge across all stages of life.”

Vision

To create a safe, informed, and supportive space where Muslim women can confidently make health decisions rooted in evidence, compassion, and faith.

Our Focus Areas

Our Focus Areas

DAHI helps women and young girls understand their bodies, make informed health decisions, and navigate every stage of life with confidence through trusted education and community engagement.

Menstrual Health

Support for understanding cycles, menstrual wellbeing, common concerns, and practical self-care.

Fertility & Pregnancy

Clear information on fertility awareness, conception, pregnancy health, and informed decision-making.

Contraception & Family Planning

Reliable, evidence-based education on contraception options and faith-conscious family planning.

Menopause & Ageing

Practical guidance on symptoms, healthy ageing, and support across later life stages.

Emotional Wellbeing

Support for mental wellbeing, stress, hormones, and healthy habits that strengthen overall resilience.

Meet Doc Adi

Meet Doc Adi

Adiyat Olamide D. (Doc Adi) is a General Practitioner with nearly 20 years of clinical experience, holding a Master of Public Health (University of Birmingham), Diplomas in Obstetrics & Gynaecology and Sexual & Reproductive Health, and membership in the British Society of Lifestyle Medicine.

She is passionate about empowering women through evidence-based medicine, Islamic principles, and holistic health education. Her work focuses on supporting women through menstruation, hormonal health, fertility, pregnancy, menopause, and emotional wellbeing.

Through clinical practice, community outreach, writing, and education, she has contributed to women’s health awareness, lifestyle medicine, and preventive care. She is also a co-founder of Bloom Women, creator of Gist with Doc Adi, and an active community health educator and speaker.

Her vision is to create a safe, faith-aligned space where women can learn, grow, and take control of their health with confidence and clarity.

Privacy Policy
