About DAHI

Every Woman Deserves Trusted Health Information

Too many women spend years living with unanswered questions about their health.

Questions about periods, fertility, pregnancy, menopause, hormones, emotional wellbeing, and the changes happening in their bodies often go unanswered—or are met with myths, misinformation, judgment, or silence.

At DAHI (Doc Adi Health Initiative), we believe women deserve better.

Founded by Dr. Adiyat Olamide Dele-Salawu, DAHI was created to provide a safe, supportive, and evidence-based space where women can access reliable health education, ask important questions, and make informed decisions about their wellbeing with confidence.

We are committed to bridging the gap between medical knowledge and everyday life by delivering practical health education that is accessible, culturally sensitive, and rooted in compassion.

Through webinars, community discussions, educational resources, and wellness initiatives, we empower women to better understand their bodies, advocate for their health, and navigate every stage of life—from adolescence and reproductive years to menopause and beyond.
