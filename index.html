About DAHI

Many women and young girls grow up without clear, reliable education about their own bodies. Questions about menstrual health, fertility, pregnancy, menopause, and emotional wellbeing are often left unanswered or surrounded by confusion and stigma. DAHI exists to change that.

The Doc Adi Health Initiative (DAHI) was created to bridge this gap through education, awareness, and community support that empowers women to understand and take control of their health.

Who We Are

DAHI (Doc Adi Health Initiative) is a women’s health education and empowerment initiative focused on providing accessible, clear, and supportive health information to women and young girls.

  • We operate with a simple belief: when women are informed, they are empowered — physically, emotionally, and socially.
  • We are based in Aberdeen and work with a growing global community.

Our Mission

“To educate, empower, and support women and young girls by providing accessible and trustworthy health knowledge across all stages of life.”

The Problem We Address

  • Lack of access to reliable women’s health education
  • Cultural stigma around discussing reproductive health
  • Widespread misinformation online and offline
  • Emotional isolation around health experiences
  • Limited support systems for young girls entering puberty and women transitioning through life stages

Founder

Doc Adi — General Practitioner & Public Health Advocate

Doc Adi is a General Practitioner with almost 20 years of clinical experience, holding an MPH (International, University of Birmingham), a Diploma of the Royal College of Obstetricians & Gynaecologists, and a Diploma in Sexual & Reproductive Health. She is a member of the British Society of Lifestyle Medicine (BSLM).

  • Founder & Coach – Co-founded Bloom Women (Aug 2024 – Feb 2025), coaching women on gut health and lifestyle transformation.
  • Writer & Educator – Creator of Gist with Doc Adi; authoring a book guiding Muslim women through puberty and reproductive health.
  • Public Health Advocate – Led community health awareness programmes on prevention and lifestyle change.
  • Community Speaker & Instructor – Delivered talks in local mosques and trained community members in Basic Life Support with BIMA.

She envisions a safe, faith-aligned space where Muslim women can learn, grow, and thrive—physically, emotionally, and spiritually.
